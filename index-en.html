About

My research uses large-scale health care data to evaluate health policy and inform its design.
I focus in particular on disparities in health care utilization and outcomes, with the aim of building evidence for more equitable and effective policy.

Research Interests
⦁ Evidence-based evaluation and design of health policy
⦁ Disparities in health care utilization and health outcomes
⦁ Health equity

Research Methods
⦁ Quantitative analysis of national health insurance claims and other administrative data
⦁ Survey design, instrument development, and administration
⦁ Qualitative interviewing and thematic analysis
⦁ Software: SAS, R, QGIS
